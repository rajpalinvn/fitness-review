How testing works

  1. 1Get the app link and the test access password from the developer.
  2. 2Open this guide and save your name.
  3. 3Open the app and sign in with your test account.
  4. 4Pick a case below.
  5. 5Do its steps.
  6. 6Mark Pass, Fail or Blocked and write a note. For Fail, also send a screenshot.
  7. 7Repeat from step 4 with the next case.

After every update, read "What's new" first, then re-test the cases it lists.
